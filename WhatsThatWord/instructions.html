What's That Word!

Objective

Guess the hidden word one letter at a time before you run out of tries!

How to Play

  • Choose a Mode
    • School Mode: Enter your own list of words separated by commas (like math,science,history) or upload a .txt file with one word per line.
    • Fun Mode: Play using a built-in set of words already included in the game.
  • Start the Game: After choosing a mode, a hidden word will appear on the screen as underscores (_ _ _ _).
  • Make a Guess: Type a single letter in the box and press Guess or press Enter.

Rules

  • You have 5 wrong tries before you lose the round.
  • Correct letters appear in their positions.
  • Wrong guesses increase your wrong count.

Winning & Losing

  • Win: Guess all the letters before reaching 5 wrong tries.
  • Lose: After 5 wrong guesses, the word is revealed.

After the Round

  • Click Replay to play again.
  • Click Home to return to the mode selection screen.

Hints

After a few wrong guesses, a Get Hint button may appear. Click it to get a clue about one of the letters in the word.

Tips

  • Guess one letter at a time (A–Z).
  • Start with common vowels like A, E, and O.
  • You can return to the main menu anytime.
